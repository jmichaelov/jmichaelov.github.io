This is my first blog post.

While I have managed some WordPress-based sites in the past (Specifically, the University of Edinburgh Linguistics and English Language Society (LangSoc) and the Undergraduate Linguistics Association of Britain (ULAB) websites), I have never written a blog post before, so I'll keep it short.

The aim of this blog is to post about things that I find academically interesting (in Cognitive Science, Linguistics, and related fields), but without needing to be as formal or polished as with most academic writing, and without necessarily making a specific argument or taking a specific stance on the relevant topic.


I'm interested in seeing how it develops over time.
